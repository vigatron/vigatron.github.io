2026 VHLIBOptimal C++17 library for fast shape detection, object counting, and outer boundary estimation.

A lightweight, C++17 library focused exclusively on identifying discrete shapes, counting objects, and extracting their spatial coordinates and external dimensions using efficient bit-packed grid scanning.
