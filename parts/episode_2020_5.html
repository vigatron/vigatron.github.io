2020 CMBoards: STM32 + FPGA Platforms with HDMI or 7" TFT Output

STM32H750 + Xilinx XC6SLX9 (Spartan-6) based embedded boards.

Retro-computing and gaming platform supporting ZX Spectrum, NES, Atari and DOS-class emulation systems.

Hardware architecture includes FPGA-based video pipeline, HDMI display interface, SDRAM subsystem, low-latency I/O handling and real-time peripheral control.
